Your content

Your photos, videos and other files stay yours. The app only moves them between devices you control, and the project's contributors never receive or access them. See the privacy policy.

Your responsibilities

  • Only sync content you have the right to copy, and only to devices you own or are authorised to use.
  • Keep your devices and network secure. Transfers are encrypted, but anyone who controls one of your paired devices can send files to, or receive files from, your others.
  • Only pair devices you own or are allowed to use, check the pairing code on both screens, and don't use the app to access other people's devices without permission.
  • Only accept files you expect. Received files aren't scanned for malware.
  • Follow the laws that apply to you and the terms of your device and app store providers.

Not a guaranteed backup

The app is designed to copy files faithfully: it verifies every file by checksum and never overwrites existing files. Even so, software has bugs and storage fails. Don't let Lucero Sync be the only copy of anything you can't afford to lose. Check your backups now and then, and follow the 3-2-1 rule: three copies, on two kinds of media, with one stored elsewhere.
